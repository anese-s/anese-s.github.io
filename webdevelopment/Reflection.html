Reflection Page

Future Aspirations

When I think about my future career, I realize that I am still figuring out exactly what kind of designer I want to become. I have always known that I wanted to do something creative, but studying graphic design has shown me that there are many different directions I can take. I used to think of design mostly as creating something that looked good, but I have learned that there is much more to it. Design is about communicating an idea and understanding how people will respond to what they see. The more I learn, the more interested I become in how creativity can be used for different purposes and audiences. I think being open to discovering what I enjoy instead of feeling like I need to have everything figured out has been an important part of my growth.

Personal Growth and Development

One thing I have learned about myself is that I enjoy variety. I like exploring different types of art, mediums, and styles, and I do not want to feel stuck doing the exact same thing all the time. At the same time, I am starting to notice the parts of graphic design that I am most drawn to, especially branding and visual identity. I like the idea of taking something that already has an identity and figuring out how to visually communicate what it represents. My experiences creating graphics for The Maneater and promotional materials for my family's hardware store have also helped me understand that every project has different needs. Working on these projects has made me more comfortable with designing for a purpose.nstead of only designing for myself. As I continue through college, I want to focus on becoming more confident in my abilities and gaining experience outside of the classroom. I know that there are still areas where I have a lot to learn, and I actually see that as something exciting rather than discouraging. I want to try internships and freelance work so I can experience working with real clients, receiving feedback, and making changes based on someone else's needs. I also want to continue building my portfolio so that it represents more than just assignments I completed for school. Eventually, I want someone to look at my work and be able to see my creativity, versatility, and the thought behind my designs.

My Career Goals

I also want my career to reflect who I am as a person. My creativity is connected to the way I see the world, and my faith has influenced the kind of messages I want to put into the world. I want to create work that can make people feel something, whether that is excitement, hope, connection, or simply curiosity. I know that my career may change as I gain more experience, and I am not expecting myself to have every step planned out yet. Right now, my goal is to keep learning, keep creating, and find opportunities that allow me to grow. Ultimately, I want graphic design to be a career where I can use something I have loved since childhood while continuing to discover what I am capable of.
