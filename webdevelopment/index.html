My Purpose

I started creating art as a child because I loved having a visual picture of the world I was creating in my head and also falling in love with the reactions others would have when I created works for them. My vision is to incorporate the love of the world and humanity into all my work. I wish to create works that inspire people to create and spread feelings of hope and unity to each other. My faith plays a big part in why I choose to create art. It shapes my perspective on the world and my drive to make a positive impact on the world through art and other media.
