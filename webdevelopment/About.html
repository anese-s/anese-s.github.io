Introduction

My name is Anese Daniel. I am a 20-year-old graphic design student, currently in my junior year at the University of Missouri. I was born on the island of Saint Kitts, where I lived until 17 before returning to the United States. My main interest is art in all of its forms, exploring new mediums and learning their origins. Beyond art, I enjoy nature walks, volunteering in my community, and helping out at Newman Church. My biggest goal is to visit all the national parks in America. I have currently only been to one, but I hope for that list to grow in the future. Along with personal goals, my biggest professional goal is to gain more experience in the graphic design field through internships and freelance work. I plan to pursue graphic design in the marketing field. I primarily want to focus on brand identity in terms of logos and eventually website building.

Education

I have previously earned an AAS in Graphic Design at Saint Charles Community College and plan to acquire my BFA in fall 2028 at the University of Missouri (MI-Z) in Graphic Design with a certificate in digital marketing.

Experience

I have worked with the local school newspaper, The maneater creating graphics such as ones featured for the upcoming documentary “the trail state”. I also have worked for my family’s business creating graphics for their hardware store promotional posters.
